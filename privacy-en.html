Kalan Privacy Policy

Effective date: DD Month YYYY

1. In short

  • Your income, expense, plan, budget, and debt records stay on your own device and in your own iCloud account. The developer has no server or account that holds them; the developer can't access them and doesn't collect them.
  • Kalan has no user account or sign-in.
  • When the app crashes, a technical crash report is sent to Google's Firebase Crashlytics service so the problem can be fixed. The report doesn't include amounts, descriptions, or account names.
  • For exchange rates, only currency codes and dates are sent to a third-party service (Frankfurter).
  • Kalan doesn't show ads, doesn't track you, and doesn't include a usage analytics tool.

2. Data controller and contact

Kalan is developed and published by Yağız Savran. In this policy, "the developer" and "data controller" refer to him.

Contact and requests: savranyagiz@gmail.com

3. What happens to which data

3.1 Your financial records

Your income, expense, plan, budget, account, card, and debt/installment records, your account and category names, and the descriptions you write are stored on your device. They aren't sent to the developer or collected by the developer. Kalan has no server that receives these records.

3.2 iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, your records sync through Apple's CloudKit service, only with the devices signed in to your own iCloud account. This data stays in your iCloud account (not in the developer's iCloud account or on a developer's server) and is processed by Apple's services; the developer can't access it. Sync is on by default and runs automatically; it isn't guaranteed to be instant. You can pause it with the "iCloud sync" switch in Settings. Pausing doesn't delete your records in iCloud. This switch doesn't affect whether diagnostic data is sent.

3.3 Error diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

When Kalan crashes, a crash report is sent to Google's Firebase Crashlytics service so the problem can be found and fixed. The report includes information such as the app version, device model, iOS version, and the technical details of the crash. The service may also use technical identifiers specific to the app installation and session/device events, and, as with any internet request, Google's servers can technically see the connection's IP address. The full list of what the service collects and how it is processed is described in Google's documentation and policy (see section 5).

Transaction amounts, descriptions, debt information, account names, limits, balances, the contents of files you export, and labels you write are never added to diagnostic data.

Diagnostic reporting is on by default and can't be turned off inside the app.

Kalan doesn't include Google Analytics or any other usage analytics tool.

3.4 Exchange rates

To show records in different currencies, rates come from Frankfurter, a third-party service that provides the European Central Bank's daily reference rates. To our knowledge, the service is delivered through Cloudflare infrastructure. As with any internet request, this service can see your device's IP address; the service states that it doesn't store IP addresses (the developer hasn't independently verified this statement).

Kalan's rate request doesn't include amounts, descriptions, or identity; only currency codes and the dates needed are sent. If all your records and your display currency match your account currency, no rate is needed and no request is made. Rates are reference rates, not bank rates.

3.5 Kalan Pro subscription

Purchases and payments are handled by Apple. Your payment details and Apple Account details aren't passed to the developer. Kalan reads whether your subscription is active on your device through Apple's StoreKit framework. The developer can see the sales and subscription reports that Apple provides through App Store Connect; Kalan doesn't add information to those reports on its own.

3.6 Notifications and export

Reminders are scheduled on your device and don't include amounts by default. Kalan asks for notification permission only when you turn on a reminder.

PDF and CSV files are generated on your device; Kalan doesn't store or sync them. You decide who receives a file and where it goes.

3.7 What we don't collect

Kalan has no user account, email, or password. Kalan doesn't show ads, doesn't track you across apps and websites, and doesn't sell personal data.

5. Third parties and international transfers

When you use Kalan, data is processed by the following services. All three operate outside Turkey, and some of them outside the EU/EEA, so related data may be transferred internationally.

  • Apple Inc. — iCloud/CloudKit (sync), App Store and subscriptions. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google LLC (Firebase Crashlytics) — crash reports. For retention, processing, and transfer safeguards, see Google's documentation: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Frankfurter and its infrastructure provider Cloudflare — exchange rates. Frankfurter: frankfurter.dev; Cloudflare privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

The developer doesn't send your personal financial data to these services. International transfers are subject to these services' own processing terms and to the safeguards required by applicable law. Personal data isn't sold or shared for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your records on your device and in your iCloud account: They stay until you delete them. The "Delete all my data" option in Settings deletes your records on this device and the Kalan data in your own iCloud account; your other synced devices receive the deletion too. This can't be undone. When other devices receive the deletion isn't guaranteed. Deleting doesn't cancel your Kalan Pro subscription; the subscription is with Apple. Device settings such as appearance, language, and notification preferences aren't affected by deletion.
  • If you delete the app: The copy on the device is removed; the copy in iCloud remains. To delete the iCloud data too, use "Delete all my data" before removing the app, or delete the Kalan data from iCloud storage management in iPhone Settings.
  • Crash reports: They are held by Google's Firebase Crashlytics service. Retention depends on Google's policy (link in section 5). The developer may not be able to find a report that belongs to you because reports don't link to your name or account; if you have a request, write to us anyway.
  • Rate requests: The developer doesn't store the IP address in rate requests. Kalan keeps downloaded rates in a cache on your device; "Delete all my data" clears that cache too.
  • Subscription records: They are held by Apple and are subject to Apple's policy.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under applicable law (KVKK Art. 11; if you are in the EU/EEA, GDPR Arts. 15–22), you have the right to:

  • Find out whether your data is processed and request information if it is,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • Know the third parties to whom data is transferred, in Turkey or abroad,
  • Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • Request erasure or destruction,
  • Object to processing, and withdraw your consent where processing relies on it,
  • If you are in the EU/EEA, request data portability and restriction of processing,
  • Request compensation if you suffer damage.

Because your financial records aren't held by the developer, you exercise most rights over them directly in the app: edit, delete, or use "Delete all my data". For other requests, write to savranyagiz@gmail.com. Say what you are asking for and which email address we can reply to; don't send amounts, account details, or other sensitive data. We reply within 30 days at the latest (KVKK Art. 13), and within one month under GDPR. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board in Turkey (kvkk.gov.tr), or to the data protection authority in the country where you live in the EU/EEA.

8. Children

Kalan isn't directed at children. Because financial records are never sent to the developer, no personal data of children is collected.

9. Security

Your records are protected on your device by Apple's system protections and in your iCloud account by Apple's security measures. Rate and diagnostic requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

10. Changes

If how the app uses data changes, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. For a meaningful change, we will also mention it in the "What's New" notes of the app update.
